Introduction
============

Strom is a free, open-source script that brings smart heating to your home. It uses weather forecasts and electricity price data to fine-tune energy use, finding a cost-effective heating schedule through convex optimization.

Key Features:
-------------
* **Smart Heating**: Automatically adjust heating based on electricity prices.
* **Weather Forecasting**: Integrate local weather forecasts for better planning.
* **Optimization**: Uses convex optimization to find the best schedule.
