Authoritative 2R2C Thermal Parameters for a Spanish Residential ApartmentAn exhaustive review of available literature and institutional databases reveals that a direct source publishing explicit 2R2C grey-box parameters for Spanish residential apartments does exist, despite the rarity of such data in standard peer-reviewed formats. While traditional academic papers often utilize higher-order models (like 3R2C) or relegate scalar datasets to supplementary files, the Horizon 2020 AmBIENCe project explicitly publishes a comprehensive database of grey-box RC parameters for Spanish multi-family typologies. Furthermore, recent 2025 peer-reviewed literature from the Catalonia Institute for Energy Research (IREC) directly calibrates R2C2 models for multi-family buildings specifically in the Barcelona C2 climate zone.
